# AI Viral Video Generator

[aiviralvideo.net](https://aiviralvideo.net/) is a browser-based AI video generator that turns photos into short videos using reference clips. Pick a template, assign photos to the performers you want to replace, then review and download the result as a 720p MP4. The generator follows the reference choreography and framing and attempts to preserve its audio.

## Wassup in a Minute AI

Make a [wassup in a minute AI video](https://aiviralvideo.net/wassup-in-a-minute-ai) with the misty hilltop dance template. Assign up to three photos to the left, center, and right dancers. Empty slots keep the original performers.

- 15 seconds, landscape (16:9), 720p MP4
- 30 credits per generation

## Deck the Halls AI

Use the [Christmas dance video maker](https://aiviralvideo.net/deck-the-halls-ai) to put yourself, a friend, or a pet in a Christmas pajama dance. Assign one or two photos to the left and right dancers. The vertical clip works for holiday greetings and duets.

- 14.42 seconds, portrait (9:16), 720p MP4
- 30 credits per generation

## How it works

1. Preview the reference clip and choose the roles you want to replace.
2. Upload one JPG, PNG, or WebP photo per selected role, up to 10 MB each. Use photos you have permission to upload, with one consenting adult or one animal per photo.
3. Generate the video, review the faces, movement, and audio, then download the MP4.
